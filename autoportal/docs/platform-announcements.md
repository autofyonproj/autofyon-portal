# Platform announcements on dealer sites

Platform admins manage the shared advertisement carousel and news ribbon at
`/admin/platform/announcements`. Changes are saved as a draft until published.
Published items appear on public dealer landing pages and minisite homepages;
they are not editable by dealer admins. Minisite subpages and admin previews do
not show this shared placement.

The carousel is a compact card on mobile and a right-side rail on desktop.
News items are horizontally scrollable links above the dealer content. Images
must use HTTPS, and links may be HTTPS URLs or same-site paths. The current
portal hero carousel remains managed separately at `/admin/platform/carousel`.
