        Pioneering design and performance.

        The BMW X5 combines a distinctive design with outstanding performance, first-class comfort, and impressive versatility. As a pioneering Sports Activity Vehicle (SAV), it is setting the benchmark both on and off-road.

        A powerfully elegant character.

        The striking twin kidney grille and defined LED headlights of the wide front end catch the eye. A character line rises from the rear doors, accentuating the vehicle’s athletic proportions. Powerful wheel arches and large alloy wheels enhance the off-road capabilities of the BMW X5 – and not just visually. At the rear, horizontal lines, sculpted bumpers, and integrated exhaust tailpipes underscore the width of the vehicle. All in all, a totally stylish and self-confident all-rounder.

        Frequently asked questions on the BMW X5.

        The BMW X5 is available as an efficient combustion engine vehicle with mild hybrid technology and as a powerful M model.

        The vehicle measures 4,935 mm long, 2,004 mm wide, and 1,765 mm high.

        Consumption and CO2 emissions.

        BMW X5 xDrive40i: Fuel consumption, combined WLTP in l/100 km: 9.2; CO2 emissions, combined WLTP in g/km: 211

        Performance data of petrol engines apply to vehicles using RON 98 fuel. Fuel consumption data apply to vehicles using reference fuels in accordance with EU Regulation 715/2007. Unleaded RON 91 and higher with a maximum ethanol content of 10% (E10) may also be used. BMW recommends RON 95 fuel. For high-performance automobiles, BMW recommends RON 98 fuel.


        Official data for fuel consumption, CO2 emissions, power consumption and electric range was determined in accordance with the prescribed measuring procedure and corresponds to European Regulation (EC) 715/2007 in the applicable version. For ranges, data determined as per WLTP takes into account any optional equipment (available on the German market in this case). For vehicles that have been newly type approved since 1 January 2021, only the official data according to WLTP exists. In addition, NEDC values are deleted from the certificates of conformity as of 1 January 2023 by EC regulation 2022/195. For more information about NEDC and WLTP measuring procedures visit www.bmw.com/wltp


        Further information about fuel consumption and official model-specific CO2 emissions of new passenger cars can be found in the "Guideline for fuel consumption, CO2 emissions and electric power consumption for new passenger cars", available free of charge at all points of sale, at the Deutsche Automobil Treuhand GmbH (DAT), Hellmuth-Hirth-Str. 1, 73760 Ostfildern-Scharnhausen, Germany, and under https://www.dat.de/CO2/.
